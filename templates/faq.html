Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 13, 2026 • Find quick answers to common questions

Is Acuwealth really free to use?

Yes. Acuwealth is completely free for all users. There are no trial periods, no hidden fees, and no credit card required to sign up or use any tracking features.

How does multi-currency conversion work?

When you record a transaction in a foreign currency, it stays in its original currency and amount. Dashboard totals and converted amounts are calculated using public exchange rates fetched from open.er-api.com, updated hourly and cached locally.

Is my financial data shared with third parties?

Never. We do not sell your data, use advertising trackers, or share your financial records with any third party. The exchange rate service only receives currency pairs, never user or account information.

How do I delete my data permanently?

You can delete individual transactions at any time. To permanently delete your entire account and all associated transactions, use the "Delete account" option in Preferences or submit a request on our Privacy Policy page.

Can I access Acuwealth on my mobile phone?

Yes. Acuwealth is fully responsive and mobile-optimized. You get the complete tracking interface, slide-out navigation, and live updates on any modern mobile browser.

Does Acuwealth work offline?

Acuwealth requires a network connection to sync your transactions securely with the server, but it includes an offline indicator to let you know if your internet connection is lost so you never lose track of unsaved actions.
