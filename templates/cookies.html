Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 • Effective date: October 13, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and browser storage are small text files or key-value entries stored on your device by websites you visit. They help sites remember your session and preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

We use only strictly necessary cookies and local storage items essential for the application to function:

Identifier Type Purpose Duration
session HTTP cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax) Stores your signed-in session state and CSRF token. Session only
acuwealth-theme localStorage Remembers your light/dark mode preference. Persistent
acuwealth-cookie-consent localStorage Remembers your cookie banner acknowledgement. 1 year

3. No Third-Party Tracking Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing beacons, or third-party tracking cookies. Your financial data is never shared with advertiser networks.
