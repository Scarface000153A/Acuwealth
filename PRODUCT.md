# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a Python and SQLite backend.

## Users

Inferred from the request: people who want to manage their personal income and expenses.

## Product Purpose

Let signed-in users record income and expenses, organize transactions by type, and review their finances in a chosen currency.

## Positioning

No distinct positioning or competitive claim was provided.

## Operating Context

Users enter income and expenses in one of several currencies and review their overall cash flow in their preferred display currency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Create an account, sign in, and sign out.
- Add income and expense transactions with a category, date, amount, and currency.
- Set a preferred display currency.
- Convert amounts and dashboard totals using exchange rates.
- New accounts default to INR, as selected by the user.
- The browser interface must use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; the server uses Python and SQLite.
- Deployment target and multi-user administrative needs are open decisions.

## Evidence on Hand

The original request specifies the product's initial feature set and technology stack. No sample financial data, logo, brand guide, market claims, or other product assets were provided. Do not invent financial performance data or user testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Keep currency conversions understandable by showing original and converted amounts.
- Keep income and expense categories distinct.
- Do not treat sample or fabricated financial data as real account activity.
- Make account access and transaction entry straightforward.
