LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 • Effective date: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

Acuwealth ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Acuwealth personal finance application.

2. Data We Collect (And What We Don't)

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect data that is strictly necessary to provide the service:

  • Account data: Your name, email address, and a salted and hashed password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Financial activity: Income and expense transactions you explicitly record (description, amount, currency, category, and date).
  • Preferences: Your preferred display currency.
  • Marketing attribution: Optional UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign) if you arrived via a link.

We do NOT collect: Bank credentials, credit card numbers, location tracking, device telemetry, or children's personal data.

3. Children's Privacy

Acuwealth is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please submit a deletion request and we will promptly remove it.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

We do not use advertising networks, social tracking pixels, or commercial analytics SDKs. The only third-party services we interact with are:

  • Exchange rates (open.er-api.com): Public exchange rate data is fetched by our server and cached locally. No user or account data is ever shared with the exchange rate provider.
  • Google Fonts: Typography stylesheets (Instrument Serif). Only your browser's standard font request is made.
  • Media CDN (CloudFront): Video demonstrations are hosted on a content delivery network. No cookies or personal identifiers are passed.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may:

  • View all of your stored transactions and account details at any time in the app.
  • Delete individual transactions instantly.
  • Request permanent deletion of your entire account and all associated transactions via our in-app Data Deletion tool or by submitting a request below.
  • Opt out of non-essential email notifications at any time.

Need your data deleted?

Signed-in users can delete their entire account and all associated financial records immediately from their Preferences page, or use the form below.
